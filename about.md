### Brief CV

Since 2019, I have lived in Newcastle-upon-Tyne in the North East of the UK. I first worked as a postdoctoral researcher, primarily with the [Supergen Energy Networks hub](https://supergenen.org/) project and the EPSRC MC2 project. In 2022, I was then funded by a [CDICE Fellowship](https://www.cdice.ac.uk/c-dice-fellowship/), then by my present [Royal Academy of Engineering Research Fellowship](https://raeng.org.uk/research-fellowships). 

My PhD was with the University of Oxford with the [Energy and Power Group](https://epg.eng.ox.ac.uk/) and I was funded by a [Clarendon Scholarship](https://www.ox.ac.uk/clarendon/). I have an M.Eng undergraduate degree (1st rank) in Engineering Science, also from the University of Oxford.
